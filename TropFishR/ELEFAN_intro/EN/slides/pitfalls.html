CAUTION

Four pitfalls

① Settings change the answer

Changing the moving-average width (MA) or the length-class width moves the estimates

② The L∞–K seesaw

“Large L∞, small K” fits about as well as “small L∞, large K”

③ Individual variation blurs peaks

When fish of the same age vary in length, peaks smear and K is easily misestimated

④ Noise still gives an answer

Even data with no growth signal yield a “plausible” K from chance peaks
